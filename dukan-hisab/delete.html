Delete your Dukan Hisab account and data

Dukan Hisab (com.jhatpatapps.dukanhisab) by Jhatpat Apps

Fastest: from the app

  1. Open Dukan Hisab and go to the Setting tab.
  2. Tap Account aur saara hisab mitayein (Delete account and data).
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. Your account and all your hisab are deleted at once.

Without the app

Email jhatpatapps.dev@gmail.com from the Google account you used in the app, with the subject "Delete my Dukan Hisab data". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google email and account ID).
  • All sales, purchases, notes, shop name and settings stored on our servers.
  • Disaster-recovery backups are overwritten within 14 weeks.

Copies you made yourself (PDF or CSV files you shared, and the weekly copy in your own Google Drive) are under your control. To remove the Drive copy: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Dukan Hisab → Delete hidden app data.
