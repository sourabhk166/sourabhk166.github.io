Privacy policy

Dukan Hisab (Android app com.jhatpatapps.dukanhisab) by Jhatpat Apps · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Dukan Hisab helps a shopkeeper write the day's sale and purchases and see weekly, monthly and yearly profit. This policy explains what data the app stores, where, and how you can delete it.

What we store

  • Your Google account email and an account ID. You sign in with the Google account already on your phone. We use it only so that your hisab belongs to you and comes back on a new phone.
  • Your hisab: the amounts you write (sales and purchases), their dates and any notes you type.
  • Shop name, only if you enter one. It is printed on your month PDF.
  • Your chosen app language.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, SMS, location, camera or files. The app requests no runtime permissions.

Where it is stored

  • Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), in Google's Mumbai, India region, under your account only. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or write it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.
  • On your phone, in the app's private storage, so the app works without internet.
  • Your own Google Drive, if you turn on the weekly copy: a file in the hidden app-data folder that only this app can read. It counts toward your Drive storage and stays under your control.
  • PDF and CSV files you export go only where you choose to send them.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share your hisab with anyone.
  • We never send messages to your customers or contacts.
  • We never lock viewing, writing or exporting your hisab behind a payment.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, the app asks for your phone's own fingerprint or screen lock. That check happens inside Android; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint, PIN or password.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the Hisab and Setting screens, and at most one full-screen ad a day, only after you finish something such as sharing a PDF or CSV (never in the first 3 days, never while you write an entry). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Your hisab is never shared with the ads SDK. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

  • Delete single entries any time; they stay in the Bin for 30 days and are then removed.
  • Delete your account and all hisab in the app: Setting → "Account aur saara hisab mitayein". This removes everything we store for your account.
  • Or request deletion on the web: delete your Dukan Hisab data.
  • Server backups used for disaster recovery are overwritten within 14 weeks.

Children

The app is meant for adults running a shop and is not directed at children under 13.
