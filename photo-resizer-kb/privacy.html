Privacy policy

Photo Resizer KB (Android app com.jhatpatapps.formphoto) by Jhatpat Apps · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Photo Resizer KB helps you resize a photo, signature or similar image to the exact pixel size and file size (KB) that an online form asks for. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Your photos stay on your phone

  • Every photo you choose or take is processed only on your device. We never upload your photos, signatures or the files the app makes to any server, and we have no server that could receive them.
  • You choose photos with Android's system photo picker, so the app only sees the one photo you pick. It does not ask for permission to read your gallery.
  • If you tap Camera, your phone's own camera app takes the picture and hands it to this app. The app does not need or request the camera permission.
  • Files you save go to Pictures › Form Photos on your phone. You can delete them at any time from your gallery. Temporary copies used while editing are kept in the app's private storage and are deleted automatically.
  • You can share a file using Android's share menu. It goes only where you choose to send it.

No account, no personal details

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, roll number or any other personal details, and we do not collect them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash information related to the ads.

This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your photos are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time under About › Ad privacy settings in the app.

White background (Google ML Kit)

If you turn on Make background white, the app finds the person in the photo with Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation. This runs entirely on your phone: the photo is not sent anywhere. The ML Kit model is downloaded once through Google Play services. To deliver and improve the model, ML Kit may send Google device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and app version), a device identifier, performance data (such as how long processing took) and error codes. It never sends the photo. Google handles this data under Google's Privacy Policy; see ML Kit data disclosure.

Language setting

The app remembers whether you chose English or Hindi, and a small counter used to space out full-screen ads. These are stored only on your device.

Children

The app is meant for adults filling in exam and job application forms. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Files you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Not a government app

Photo Resizer KB is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorised by any government body, exam board or recruitment agency. Exam names are used only to describe the size presets.
